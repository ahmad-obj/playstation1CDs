# Original PlayStation gameplay imagery

These eighteen captures come from the original PlayStation game pages at [PlayStation DataCenter](https://psxdatacenter.com/), retrieved 2026-09-25. They are original PS1 gameplay or rendered cinematics, not remake footage or generated approximations. Each candidate set was reviewed as a montage; selection favors atmosphere, spatial composition, and recognizable environments rather than menus.

The site reuses screenshot sets across regional game pages. Metal Gear Solid, R4, and Resident Evil 2 use the PAL screenshot sets actually linked from the requested Japanese/North American pages. Final Fantasy VII is PAL. Tekken 3 and Wipeout are North American; Wipeout is specifically SCUS-94301. Disc regions and screenshot regions therefore need not match. No region-specific visual alteration was made.

Images were converted mechanically with ImageMagick to quality-90 WebP at their original source dimensions. Original letterboxing, pixels, and in-game overlays are preserved; no artificial detail, cropping, or retouching was introduced. Artwork and trademarks remain with their respective owners. These citations provide provenance, not commercial redistribution rights.

The table below records the original eighteen captures. Fifteen captures added with the expanded collection do not yet have their exact source-image URLs recorded; their local filenames and captions are in `src/media-data.ts`.
