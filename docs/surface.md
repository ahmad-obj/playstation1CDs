# Collection surface

Mode: Experience. Creative decisions delegated explicitly in the original brief.

## Direction contract
THESIS: PLAY / BACK presents six game discs as an oversized, tactile object collection. The gallery itself is the first screen; no introductory marketing screen.

OWN-WORLD: Warm console-plastic gray, graphite ink, chartreuse catalog labels, condensed industrial display lettering, fine rules, precise numbering and real disc scans. A soft elliptical shadow grounds each disc.

STORY: Recognize the discs, slide the collection, select an artifact, turn it over, read a short memory of the game. The index and about surfaces complete the exhibition.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Small wordmark and navigation across the top, huge PLAY / BACK lettering spanning the width, a brief two-line exhibition description at its right. Three full/partial physical discs span the central lower viewport. A selected game title, browse controls, and a six-slot timeline anchor the bottom.

FORM: Third grounded direction: the listening-room contact sheet. Alternatives considered: console memory-card browser, archival storage sleeve, listening-room contact sheet, Japanese game advertisement, disc-mastering studio, jewel-case shelf, museum acquisition ledger. Seed bf4d0bce. The sneaker archive challenger reinforces object-selection continuity; the shader portal reinforces quality of reflected light. Both lose on original PlayStation identification to authentic disc artwork. No visual costume borrowed.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: The drag carousel has inertia, directional disc roll, pointer-led tilt and reflective light. Selection rearranges the same scene into a close inspection of a printed front and black polycarbonate reverse.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Implementation plan
1. React/TypeScript shell, structured six-game data, local assets and fonts.
2. WebGL disc scene with annular geometry, face texture, thin edge, physical hub, dark reverse shader, studio environment and demand rendering.
3. Gallery controls and drag, touch, keyboard and wheel input with inertia; accessible HTML alternatives.
4. Inspection, index, about, sound toggle and session-persisted preference.
5. Desktop, tablet, mobile, reduced-motion and fallback browser verification; production build and corrective visual QA.
