# PLAY / BACK

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Delegated by the user's autonomous-build brief. React, TypeScript, Vite and Three.js, with self-hosted assets and a static production build.

## Users
Visitors exploring a creative developer's portfolio and people who remember the original PlayStation.

## Product Purpose
An interactive exhibition of iconic original PlayStation game discs. Visual impact, tactile exploration, responsive composition and polished motion are the primary success criteria.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Browse a curated collection by dragging, scrolling, keyboard or explicit controls. Inspect each physical disc and its reverse, discover concise contextual information, switch to a collection index, and read exhibition credits. Real games and artwork; no ROMs or emulation. Original composition inspired by the quality of the supplied A24 reference.

## Product Principles
The artifact leads. Interaction is the content. Motion preserves spatial continuity. Mobile gets an intentional composition. Reduced motion and keyboard alternatives are first-class.

## Evidence on Hand
Original request and https://a24.raviklaassens.com/. Disc scans will be sourced with provenance recorded. The exhibition name and six-game selection are creative decisions, not user-supplied facts.
